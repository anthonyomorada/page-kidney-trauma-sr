# Systematic Review Screening and Manuscript Review Worksheet

This worksheet is to be used by independent reviewers to determine inclusion or exclusion of studies identified in the search strategy. This document includes the inclusion and exclusion criteria used at both the abstract screening and full manuscript review stages. 

## Abstract Screening Worksheet

This section is for **abstract screening only**. Reviewers must rely solely on the title and abstract content, without accessing the full manuscript. Please check all boxes that apply.

### Inclusion Criteria

- [ ] Involves human subject(s)
- [ ] Traumatic etiology described (including iatrogenic trauma)
- [ ] Mentions Page kidney or Page phenomenon
- [ ] Original clinical case(s) or case series

### Exclusion Criteria

- [ ] Non-traumatic etiology only (e.g., tumor, cyst, spontaneous hemorrhage)
- [ ] No mention of Page kidney or Page phenomenon
- [ ] Review article or editorial without primary patient data
- [ ] Non-human or in vitro study

### Final Decision

Mark **Include** if at least one inclusion criterion is met and **none** of the exclusion criteria are selected.
- [ ] Include
- [ ] Exclude

## Manuscript Screening Worksheet

This section applies to **full-text manuscript screening** following abstract inclusion. Articles unavailable due to licensing or access limitations are automatically excluded. Reviewers must evaluate full manuscripts. In the case of case series, inclusion is valid if at least one patient meets eligibility.

### Inclusion Criteria

- [ ] Case report, case series, or observational study involving **human subjects**
- [ ] Trauma-related Page kidney, including **iatrogenic injury**
- [ ] Explicit mention of Page kidney or Page phenomenon
- [ ] Patient-level clinical details sufficient for data extraction

### Exclusion Criteria

- [ ] Non-human or in vitro study
- [ ] No mention of Page kidney or Page phenomenon
- [ ] Non-traumatic etiology only (e.g., tumor, cyst, spontaneous hemorrhage)
- [ ] Review, editorial, or commentary without primary case data
- [ ] Manuscript inaccessible for review (e.g., license restrictions)

### Final Decision

Mark **Include** if at least one inclusion criterion is met and **none** of the exclusion criteria are selected.
- [ ] Include
- [ ] Exclude
